About FinSight

Public SEC filings contain valuable financial and insider transaction information, but they can be difficult to understand. FinSight makes selected company data easier to find and explore.

Who is it for?

Retail investors, students, journalists, and anyone curious about public companies.

Where does the data come from?

We use StockFit's API, which organizes information sourced from SEC filings. We display selected company details, annual financials, and reported insider transactions when those endpoints are available.

Important note

Reported insider transactions are not proof of illegal insider trading. FinSight is an educational research tool and does not provide investment advice.
